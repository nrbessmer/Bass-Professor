bass-professor-apps-script.js with your actual Google Drive folder URL:

  const DOWNLOAD_LINK = "https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/YOUR_FOLDER_ID_HERE";

To get your folder ID:
  1. Open the Google Drive folder containing the BassProfessor.zip
  2. Click Share → Copy link
  3. The URL looks like: https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1aBcDeFgHiJkLmNoPqRsTuVw
  4. Paste the full URL as DOWNLOAD_LINK

Make sure the folder sharing is set to "Anyone with the link can view."
